Merge Defense

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Overview

Merge Defense respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is handled when you play the game and how optional in-app purchases are processed.

Data We Collect

Merge Defense does not collect or transmit personal information to our own servers. Your game progress—including level completions, coins, gems, stars, and settings—is stored locally on your device. We do not operate a game account system or send this locally stored game data to us or to third parties.

In-App Purchases

Merge Defense may offer optional in-app purchases. Payments are processed by Google Play or, where applicable, the relevant app store under its own terms and privacy policy. We use RevenueCat to validate and manage purchase receipts. RevenueCat may process purchase-related information, such as anonymized transaction or receipt data, to determine purchase status. It is not intended to receive your name, email address, or other direct personal identifiers from us.

For more information, review RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Analytics and Advertising

Merge Defense does not include third-party analytics SDKs such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Amplitude, and does not include advertising SDKs. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant data practices.

Children’s Privacy

Merge Defense may be played by users of different ages, including children. The game is designed not to collect personal information from users. If you believe that personal information has inadvertently been provided to the developer, please contact us through the developer contact details on the game’s Google Play listing so the concern can be reviewed.

Data Security

Game progress is stored on your device. Its protection depends in part on your device’s security settings and access controls. Because we do not maintain a server-side copy of this game progress, we cannot restore locally stored progress if it is deleted or the device is lost.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored game data remains on your device until it is removed by you, the game, or your device’s operating system—for example, when you clear the game’s storage or uninstall it. Purchase records may be retained by the relevant app store or purchase service according to its own policies and legal obligations.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the game changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when changes are made. Please review this page periodically.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the developer using the support or developer contact details shown on the Merge Defense listing in Google Play.

By playing Merge Defense, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to read this Privacy Policy.
